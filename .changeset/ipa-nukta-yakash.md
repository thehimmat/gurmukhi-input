`toIPA` reads nukta letters as their own sounds (ਸ਼ ʃ, ਖ਼ x, ਗ਼ ɣ, ਜ਼ z, ਫ਼ f, ਲ਼ ɭ) instead of the base letter plus a stray schwa, reads precomposed nukta letters (U+0A36 …) instead of dropping them, and reads yakash as a subjoined ਯ (ਬਿਗੵਾਪ੍ਤਿ → bɪgjaːpt̪ɪ). `phoneticKey` is unchanged.
